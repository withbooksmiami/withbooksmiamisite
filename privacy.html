Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

With Books (“With Books,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit or use withbooksmiami.com (the “Website”), purchase products or subscriptions, or otherwise interact with With Books, and how we use that information.

By using the Website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as information that is necessary to process your orders and provide our services.

Depending on how you interact with With Books, this may include:

Contact Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Shipping address
  • Billing address
  • Telephone number, if provided

Account and Order Information

  • Products and subscriptions purchased
  • Order history
  • Subscription information
  • Shipping information
  • Customer service communications

Reading Preferences

If you participate in the With Books mystery-book subscription, we may collect information you provide through our questionnaire or other interactions with us, including:

  • Favorite books and authors
  • Genres and subjects you enjoy
  • Genres, authors, or subjects you prefer to avoid
  • Your reading habits
  • Your responses to questions about your interests and tastes
  • Other information you voluntarily provide to help us understand what you like to read

This information is collected primarily so that we can curate a mystery book specifically for you.

Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment processors. With Books does not intend to store complete payment card numbers on its own systems.

Your payment information is handled according to the privacy policies and security practices of the payment processor used to complete your transaction.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Process and manage subscriptions
  • Ship books and other products
  • Curate and select mystery books for subscribers
  • Understand your reading preferences
  • Improve future book selections based on previous selections and purchase history
  • Communicate with you about your orders and subscriptions
  • Respond to customer service requests
  • Send optional marketing communications when you have agreed to receive them
  • Maintain and improve the Website and our services
  • Protect the security and integrity of our Website and business
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We will not use information collected through your With Books reading questionnaire for unrelated purposes simply because we have collected it.

If our use of personal information changes materially, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notice or consent required by applicable law.

3. Your Mystery Book Profile

The heart of the With Books subscription is curation.

When you provide information about your reading preferences, we use that information to develop an understanding of your tastes and select books that we believe you may enjoy.

We may retain information about your previous selections and orders so that future selections can be more thoughtful and personalized.

Our recommendations are subjective. Providing detailed information about your preferences does not guarantee that every book will be a perfect match.

4. Information We Share

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate With Books and provide products or services to you. These may include:

  • Payment processors
  • Shipping and delivery providers
  • Website and ecommerce service providers
  • Email service providers
  • Other vendors that help us operate our business

We only intend to provide these parties with information reasonably necessary for them to perform the services they provide to With Books.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a valid legal process, protect our rights or property, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the safety of our customers or others.

5. Cookies and Tracking

At this time, With Books does not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies to track visitors across the Website.

The Website or third-party services used to operate it may nevertheless use technologies that are necessary for basic functionality, security, shopping-cart functionality, authentication, payment processing, or other essential website operations.

6. Marketing Communications

With Books may occasionally send optional emails about books, events, subscriptions, new offerings, or other With Books activities.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option included in a marketing email or by contacting us at:

withbooksmiami@gmail.com

Opting out of marketing communications does not prevent us from sending transactional or service-related communications that are necessary to fulfill an order, manage a subscription, process a payment, or otherwise provide a service you have requested.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes, we may delete or securely dispose of it.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures appropriate to the nature of the information we maintain to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable exceptions
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications
  • Request information about how your personal information is collected or used

To make a privacy-related request, contact:

withbooksmiami@gmail.com

Please provide enough information for us to reasonably identify your account or request.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests in order to protect customer information.

If applicable law provides additional rights or procedures, we will process your request according to those requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

The Website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at withbooksmiami@gmail.com.

11. Third-Party Websites and Services

The Website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services, technology, or legal obligations change.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to periodically review this page.
